Small movements during an HR-pQCT scan can affect measurements of bone microstructure. This study develops deep convolutional neural networks to support motion grading and image-quality assessment.

The mechanobiology connection starts with reliable images: comparing local tissue change across time requires distinguishing biological differences from acquisition artefacts.

This work illustrates how machine learning can address a practical measurement problem in medical imaging.

[Read the motion-grading paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bone.2022.116607) or [explore the MotionScoreHRpQCT code](https://github.com/wallematthias/MotionScoreHRpQCT).
