Intervertebral discs distribute spinal loads while allowing movement. As their matrix and structure deteriorate, the way they carry those loads also changes. We study this relationship between tissue degeneration and mechanical function, from loading across the spine to the local environment of disc cells and extracellular matrix.

Our research combines medical imaging and computational mechanics to investigate how changes in disc structure redistribute forces and deformation. We ask which mechanical conditions are associated with degeneration, and how tissue deterioration may reinforce those conditions. Connecting physiological spinal loading with local tissue mechanics is central to this work.
